About me

Since 2015 I have run Pan KuBaka, a YouTube channel about Nintendo and games. For several years I wrote and filmed as a games journalist at Filmweb, Poland's biggest film and games site, and interviewed the creators of The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom at Nintendo of Europe HQ.

Today I run social media for the ENDORFY and Krux brands, and take on video projects on the side: reels, event coverage, hosting and editing. I know the industry from the inside, so I know what works with an audience.
